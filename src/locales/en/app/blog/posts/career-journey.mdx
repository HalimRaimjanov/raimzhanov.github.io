My work at MBulak has evolved from backend development to leading an engineering team. Each role has added a new perspective on how reliable financial software is planned, built and maintained.

## Engineering foundations

Working with SQL procedures, Python integrations and financial services taught me to consider data accuracy, system performance and maintainability together.

## Leading the team

As a Team Lead, I combine technical decisions with delivery planning and support for engineers. Code reviews, clear priorities and constructive feedback are central to this work.

## What I have learned

Good engineering depends on communication as well as code. Discussing requirements early and making technical decisions explicit helps the team solve problems and deliver with confidence.
