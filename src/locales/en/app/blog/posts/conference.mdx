I attended Firebird Conference 2025 in Moscow to learn from database specialists and exchange practical experience with developers and architects.

## Technical discussions

The sessions covered Firebird development, database performance, security and architecture. Discussions about Firebird 6.0 offered a view of the project's direction.

## Database administrator certification

During the conference, I passed the Firebird Database Administrator certification exam. The assessment gave me an opportunity to validate my database administration knowledge.

## Takeaways

The event provided practical ideas for working with databases and a chance to discuss shared engineering challenges with the Firebird community.
